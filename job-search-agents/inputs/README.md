# Inputs

Place your career data here before running the agents.

## Required files

### `resume.md`
Your current resume in plain text or markdown.
- Include all roles, dates, responsibilities, and any metrics you have
- Don't worry about formatting — just get the content in
- If you have multiple versions, paste the most complete one

### `linkedin.md`
Your current LinkedIn profile content.
- Paste your headline, about section, and each experience entry
- Include your skills list if you have one

### `target_roles.md`
What you're targeting. Include:
- Job titles you're open to (be specific — "Senior Product Manager, B2B SaaS" not "PM")
- Industries or sectors
- Company stage/size preferences (e.g. Series B-D startups, Fortune 500)
- Location preferences / remote vs in-office
- Anything you want to move toward or away from

### `job_descriptions.md`
Paste 2-4 actual job descriptions you're interested in (used by Agent 3).
Separate each with `---` and add a label like `## Company / Role`.

## Optional files

### `achievements.md`
Any additional context that might not be obvious from your resume:
- Projects you're proud of but undersold
- Metrics you remember but didn't put on your resume
- Context about company sizes, team sizes, budgets
- Awards, publications, speaking engagements
- Side projects or open source work

## Notes

- Files are read as plain text — markdown formatting is fine but not required
- The agents won't modify your input files
- All outputs go to the `outputs/` folder
- You can re-run any agent after updating your inputs — it will overwrite previous outputs
